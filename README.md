**RRNS Secret Sharing & Paillier Homomorphic Encryption — Python**

This project was developed to explore how Secret Sharing schemes and Paillier Homomorphic Encryption can be integrated to improve the protection and management of sensitive data in distributed environments.

The goal was to simulate how a secret can be securely divided into multiple shares, encrypted, refreshed, and reconstructed while analyzing different security scenarios, including the potential theft of a share.

The implementation includes algorithms for secret and share generation, homomorphic encryption, share refreshing, and secret reconstruction. Through these simulations, the project investigates how combining these techniques can strengthen data protection and reduce the risks associated with managing sensitive information.

The work also examines the trade-offs between security, computational complexity, and share management, with potential applications in cloud computing, distributed systems, and privacy-sensitive domains such as healthcare and finance.

**Technologies and concepts:** Python, Secret Sharing, RRNS, Paillier Homomorphic Encryption, Modular Arithmetic, Cryptographic Simulation, Information Security.
